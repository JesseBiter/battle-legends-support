Common questions

  • Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
  • Can I buy Crystals? No. Crystals are earned by playing. There are no real money purchases.
  • Is Online Battle really online? Not yet. Online Battle uses computer controlled opponents and is labeled as simulated in the game.
  • How do I start over? Go to Settings, then Reset Progress.

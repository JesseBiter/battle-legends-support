Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Battle Legends Arena does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Saved on your device only. Your progress (username, characters, Crystals, level, settings and match history) is saved on your own device and is never uploaded to a server.
  • Nothing extra. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Crystals are a fictional in-game currency that cannot be bought with real money.
  • Offline opponents. Online Battle currently uses computer controlled opponents that run on your device. No information is sent over the internet.
  • Deleting your data. You can delete all of your data at any time with Settings, then Reset Progress, or by deleting the app.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address and shown in the app.
